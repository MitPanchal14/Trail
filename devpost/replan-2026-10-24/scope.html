Trail helps you start and return to difficult work with little administration, while making accumulated effort visible as a journey.

First on opening: your next action. The landscape supports it.

One repeatable journey

For a student or builder working across several sessions:

Choose→Focus→Pause / complete→Return & resume

A short optional “When I'm back…” instruction preserves your place. Recorded effort extends a daily journey; completed actions establish recognizable checkpoints in a lasting goal landscape. Exact reward rules remain to be decided.

Now — approved submission boundary
  • Clear next action with saved context and Start/Resume.
  • Calm focus screen; optional slim floating companion where feasible.
  • Immediate pause, optional note, explicit completion.
  • Daily route and larger-goal landscape with understandable visual progress.
  • Manual control, optional editable AI assistance and persistent records.
Confirmed refinement — automatic background reminders

Remind users about neglected important work in the morning/daytime, in another tab and after Trail closes. Exact threshold, time/window, frequency, browser support and minimal remote data remain for the product/technical contract. Explicit opt-in; no phone calls. Occasional encouragement remains a separate enhancement.

Cut — monitoring and extra administration

No automatic observation of other apps, compulsory stopping explanations, mandatory AI processing or large floating dashboard. Time alone does not prove useful work.

Confirmed visual refinement

The existing pixel graphics are retired from the new direction. Stripe and Linear guide a light, minimal interface with selective colour, careful hierarchy and a new graphical journey. Exact visuals remain subject to review.

The proof

Work on one real task, pause, reopen Trail and resume from the saved instruction. Complete an action and recognize its contribution to the route and goal. Observe the friction and clarity rather than assuming productivity improvement.
