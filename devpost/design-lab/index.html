What to borrow from task apps

Things · calm hierarchy and progressive detail

Task details unfold when needed; Today and Evening are separated; Slim Mode reduces the sidebar. Apply that to a focused next-action panel.

Official features
Todoist · quick capture and a useful Today view

Natural-language capture and separate planning/history views. A completion archive is familiar functionality, so the mountain must provide useful contextual return.

Official features
TickTick · separate capture, focus, and reflection

Voice capture, task-detail views, Pomodoro, and statistics already exist. Keep their jobs distinct rather than putting them all on Today.

Official features
Habitica · gamification already exists

RPG rewards make habit tracking playful. Our distinctive hypothesis is context-preserving return with a persistent work landscape, not merely pixel styling.
