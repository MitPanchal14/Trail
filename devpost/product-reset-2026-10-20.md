## Proposed hypothesis, not approved scope

A personal work studio that remembers the state of real projects and turns completed work into a visible body of accomplishment. Existing timer, history, recovery and data safety can remain underneath a new interaction model. Do not compete by building a general-purpose agent inside a to-do form.

Proposed loop: capture a messy thought/context with very little ceremony; AI prepares a reversible next move; user works; a short completion or supplied output updates a project work surface; interruption leaves a precise return point; returning opens that context directly. AI should remove typing/replanning and preserve blockers, rather than produce more mandatory subtasks or motivational text. Suggestions and external actions have different permission boundaries.

Visual candidate: a crafted workbench/document-like project surface with expressive typography, restrained controls, readable spatial hierarchy and a persistent visual collection of real outputs. Completion adds a concrete named result/output tile into the project composition, with a brief transition and visible before/after. A larger project milestone changes the composition meaningfully. Avoid giant decorative scenes, generic metric boxes, compulsory chat, constant confetti, fabricated artifacts, guilt streaks and time-based achievement credit. Reduced motion and Undo must have clear equivalents. This is one candidate, not a final design decision.

Uploaded/shared links and artifacts may document actual results; self-reported completion remains clearly self-reported. The app cannot observe external work automatically without specific supported integrations/permissions. Do not propose broad background surveillance or pretend a browser can read other apps. Pick one capture route around the user's real workflow before adding integrations.

## Working sequence

1. Establish the user's persistence failure conditions and verify them with fictional records. Expose saving/recovery status where needed; never clear real records.
2. Learn one real day: where work arrives, actual work tools, interruption point and desired assistance. Choose one meaningful capture route and one kind of accomplishment.
3. Compare two static/interactive visual directions using the same realistic project, including empty, active, interrupted, returned and completed states. Judge friction and information hierarchy as well as appearance. Keep public release intact.
4. Agree the revised core loop and scope before changing approved scope/PRD/spec together. Build one end-to-end prototype rather than reskinning every route first.
5. Test effort to capture, ability to find next step, visible truthful progress, recoverability, mobile/keyboard/reduced motion and actual return behavior. Public AI protection/hosting/data services require their own concrete scope and cost choices.

## User clarification — morning readiness

User tested on a phone in Brave, apparently normal browsing. They now report work stayed after reload and attributed this to caching. Treat this as reported reload persistence, not a reproduced data-loss defect or proof of persistence after app exit/OS eviction. Trail currently uses browser-local storage, distinct from page cache; no phone/laptop sync exists.

Desired daily experience: wake up with tasks ready, know where to start immediately, and continue with low effort. User imagines connected phone/web experiences. Avoid stress from unfinished work and the additional burden of maintaining a productivity app. The supplied example describes the desired day rather than current task sources; that remains the key next discovery question.

Refined candidate promise: open Trail and know the next useful move, with unfinished context carried forward and a small adjustable daily plan prepared from known work. Prepare suggestions without silently committing, rescheduling external events or claiming knowledge of unconnected tools. Distinguish capture, suggestion, commitment and completion. Proposed interaction: morning briefing with one recommended starting action and a brief reason; Start / change / adjust available time; later return opens saved context; supplied completion yields an immediate readable change in a persistent project composition. Visual workbench remains a candidate pending prototype review, not approved final scope.

Phone/laptop continuity needs a separately agreed identity/sync/backend design and failure handling; a responsive website alone does not provide it. Native app and many integrations are not prerequisites to validate the morning-readiness loop. Choose the first actual task source before designing integrations or AI autonomy.
