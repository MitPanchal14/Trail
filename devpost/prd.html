Explore the agreed colour rule

Colour follows completion.

This is an illustrative state diagram, not the final app artwork or recorded user activity.

Unstarted: natural terrain and green trees, a starting point, and a clear first action. Completion brings additional light and colour; no fabricated progress.

Climbing motions during focus are temporary animation. Completed subtasks earn checkpoints; explicit goal completion earns the summit glow. Quiet days preserve earned colour. Adding/reordering subtasks keeps earned checkpoints stable; Undo reverses only the mistaken completion’s colour and counts. Reopening retains colour and summit glow, with a clear Reopened status and unfinished next action.

Chosen direction: bento + pixel world

Clean, readable cards organize the next action, saved context, and task controls around a generous natural pixel landscape. Layered or isometric terrain, trees, paths, checkpoints, and a small climber carry the gamification. Keep ordinary text readable; focus mode simplifies the layout to the current action, countdown, and mountain view.

Today · begin or resume

One next action leads, with saved context, editable suggested duration, Start/Resume, and a prominent mountain preview. The day plan stays secondary.

When empty

Create a task or choose existing work. No plan is not a perfect-completion day.

Check it works

The next action is immediately identifiable. Selecting another action selects the right mountain and saved context.

Mountains · explore your larger goals

A range of large tasks. Select one to inspect subtasks, earned checkpoints, and saved place. Select checkpoints to revisit what was recorded.

When paused

The current action remains unfinished. Its resting place and supplied context survive another visit.

Check it works

Completing a subtask adds local colour once. Only confirming the goal complete illuminates the whole mountain.

Progress · understand recorded activity

A yearly grid uses completed-subtask count for intensity, with a separate marker for finishing the day's committed plan. Select a day for actions, completed/planned count, focus time, and saved places. You can adjust the plan; removed or deferred commitments remain visibly labeled in daily history and do not count as completed.

When history is missing

No backfilled activity. Distinguish pre-use/future dates from days with no recorded completions.

Check it works

Pauses do not darken a completion square. Reports describe facts, not “productivity doubled.” Removing unfinished commitments cannot silently earn the all-done marker; an empty plan earns none.

Today's focus mode · one action and a countdown

Inspect the suggested duration, edit if desired, then click Start or Resume. Today automatically enters focus mode: task, countdown, and close-up climber; wider planning recedes. Complete early, extend, or save a place. Exiting restores the regular Today layout.

At zero or interruption

Zero requests an outcome; it never auto-completes. Closing the tab preserves the unfinished place and pauses the countdown at its last saved position. On return, Resume continues the remaining time; the absence earns no recorded time.

Check it works

Countdown expiry adds no checkpoint. Reopening recovers the task and remaining commitment without starting automatically. Repeated completion does not duplicate records.

New task · speak, inspect, review

Speak or type → correct transcript → request breakdown → review goal, finish condition, all subtasks, and suggested durations → edit and accept. Available from Today and Mountains.

If voice or AI fails

Keep typing usable and preserve entered text. Offer retry or manual editing; do not label a fabricated plan as live AI output.

Check it works

Nothing begins automatically before review. An accepted large task creates a persistent mountain and steps.

Approved boundary

The complete loop

Reviewed speech/text creation, daily planning, next action, countdown focus, saved places, mountain colour rewards, calendar history, simple factual reports, and deployment.

This is broader than the original tiny experiment. Build and verify the work-and-return loop before polishing secondary reports.

Deferred

Keep the boundary.

Recurring routines, desktop widgets, sharing/social feeds, teams, rich analytics, XP, and free-roaming 3D gameplay.

The app does not perform research, observe other apps, or verify attention. Dimensional terrain expresses history; it is not a separate game.

Approved defaults · October 15, 2026

Saved unfinished work leads Today; otherwise use today's committed order. Commit the draft day plan explicitly. Pause/Continue controls stop the timer; Save my place exits focus. Use grid bands 0, 1, 2–3, 4–5, 6+. Archive removed accepted steps. Correct mistaken goal completion revokes erroneous summit light; Reopen preserves a genuinely earned summit.

Dusk is the default: evening green surfaces, pale text, sage buttons with dark text and localized golden completion light. The original bento and pixel mountain remain; saved positions earn no light.
